Questions, answered

The short version.

Is this SEO audit really free?

Yes. Single-page audits with a full A+ to F grade are free. Starter (₹99/month) unlocks keyword ideas, rank checks and generators; Pro (₹799/month) adds full-site crawl, compare, the AI article writer and rank tracking.

How is the grade calculated?

Your page is checked against 20+ rules from Google's public Search Essentials: titles, meta tags, headings, speed, mobile, schema and more. Scores map to grades A+ to F.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Paste any URL in the box above and get your grade in about a minute. Nothing to install, just your email to run the free audit.

Does it work for any website?

Yes, any public page: blogs, shops, portfolios, business sites. If Google can see it, we can audit it.
