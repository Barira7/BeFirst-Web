Pricing FAQ

Everything you need to know about plans.

Can I use BeFirst SEO for free?

Yes. Single-page SEO audits with a full A+ to F grade are completely free with email signup. No credit card required.

What does the Starter plan include?

Starter (₹99/month) adds keyword ideas, rank checks, brand visibility check, generators and outlink/broken-link leads on top of everything in Free.

What does the Pro plan include?

Pro (₹799/month) adds full-site crawl (up to 100 pages), side-by-side competitor compare, AI article writer with FAQ schema, rank tracking over time, and unlinked-mention finder.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. Cancel anytime and your access continues until the end of your billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, credit/debit cards and net banking. Plans are billed monthly in INR.

Do you offer refunds?

If you're not satisfied within the first 7 days of a paid plan, email us via LinkedIn and we'll issue a full refund.
