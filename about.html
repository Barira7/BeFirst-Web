Why it exists

Most SEO tools charge ₹5,000–₹25,000/month for checks that boil down to Google's own public rules — Search Essentials. BeFirst runs those same checks and gives you the results for free.

How the grade works

Your page is checked against 20+ rules drawn directly from Google's public documentation: titles, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text, page speed, mobile-friendliness, structured data, and more. Each pass or fail feeds into a score that maps to a grade from A+ to F. No magic, no proprietary black box — just Google's rules applied mechanically.

What's free vs paid

Single-page audits with a full grade are free (email signup required). Paid plans unlock multi-page crawls, competitor comparisons, keyword research, rank tracking, and the AI article writer. The free tier isn't a teaser — it's the core product.

The philosophy

If you can fix it yourself, you shouldn't need an agency. BeFirst gives you the diagnosis; you decide whether to fix it in-house or hire someone. Either way, you know exactly what needs doing.
